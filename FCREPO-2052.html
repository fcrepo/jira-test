Michael Durbin, 2016-06-09 18:03 UTC

I haven't had time to look at this PR, and won't today, but do have a moment to try to explain. I think this is in response to the fact that we don't recommend the use of leveldb due to the fact that if you run out of memory (or file handles) it corrupts your database. Some people don't read the whole manual before proceeding with a software installation, and the kinds of problems they get in to with levelDB will only appear after they've ingested a lot of stuff into the repository.

Forcing someone to make an informed decision about their backend database is preferable to defaulting to one that's likely to leave them with problems down the road.

Andrew Woods, 2016-06-09 18:07 UTC

Thanks, Michael Durbin... exactly!
I would also add that this PR/ticket is designed to still allow administrators to use the existing LevelDB configuration if they so choose. The point being that administrators will now have to "choose" their backend, as opposed to being given one out of the box that has potential durability issues.
