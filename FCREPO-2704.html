Description

For NonRDFResources that have an "acl:accessControl" triple in their metadata, acl link header is missing. To resolve, the acl:accessControl triple must be extracted from the resource/fcr:metadata and returned in the response header. c.f.https://fcrepo.github.io/fcrepo-specification/#link-rel-acl
