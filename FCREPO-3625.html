Description

This is a regression of FCREPO-3358 Closed .

Basically stand Fedora up inside Tomcat and try to browse to http://localhost:8080/fcrepo/
(assuming you named the war fcrepo.war)

You'll get a 500 Server Error due to changes related to FCREPO-3598 Closed .

In Tomcat when you browse to /fcrepo/ the request.getServletPath() returns "/index.html". Which causes the logic to fail.
