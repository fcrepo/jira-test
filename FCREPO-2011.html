Michael Durbin, 2016-08-09 15:39 UTC

I had an issue that manifested similarly. In the full fedora logs there was a BROKEN PIPE exception and I worked with networking to try to address the issue. We couldn't, but we found a workaround that was suitable as as stopgap until the Modeshape5-based fedora.

In our infinispan configuration we switched from a pool to a simple-connection....

<jdbc:simple-connection connection-url="jdbc:mysql://$

{fcrepo.ispn.mysql.host:localhost}

:$

{fcrepo.ispn.mysql.port:3306}

...

This prevented the idle timeouts. It seems that the infinispan connection pooling doesn't validate connections so when one has timed out, you get an exception. It didn't appear to corrupt the DB if it occurred during a write, but I couldn't be sure.

Not using pooling means that there's a new connection opened for every request. This works in our case because our traffic is super light. I can easily see how under heavy load this workaround would cause a different problem (running out of handles for not-yet-cleaned-up TCP connections to the database).
