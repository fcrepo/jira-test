Description

In a similar fashion to the setup detailed in: https://jira.duraspace.org/browse/FCREPO-2188

...this ticket is to address the situation where resource namespaces are lost:

  • After /fcr:restore of the plantpatents dataset, open a tab with the following resource:
    http://localhost:8080/rest/62/44/53/d5/624453d5-e62a-43ff-b934-a04ab36acc75

Then go through the export/import cycle, and open a different tab with that same resource:
http://localhost:8080/rest/62/44/53/d5/624453d5-e62a-43ff-b934-a04ab36acc75

! Notice the difference... namespaces changed!
