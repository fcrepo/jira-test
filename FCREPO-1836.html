Description

This task is to enable fixity checks on Federated content.

A couple of use cases:

  • resources in our digital preservation store (Archvematica AIP store), as noted above
  • large files/datasets that we don't want to store in F4 itself
    The first is speculative, but the second reflects the way we currently store a lot of data with F3 and at the moment have no checksums or fixity checking.
