Description

A user may wish to reindex their fcrepo repository without having to drop the database or even drop or truncate database tables manually.

One straightforward approach would be to provide a flag to be unused on a one-off basis one startup.  For example, 

-Dfcrepo.rebuild.on.startup=true

On startup, when this flag is present, all indices would be rebuilt.
