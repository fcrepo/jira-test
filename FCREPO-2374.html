Description

I've noticed a difference in output between 4.7.0 and 4.7.1-rc1. Although there are minor and (I think) inconsequential differences to how the turtle is formatted, the main thing I see is that string datatypes specified during ingest are stripped in the output of 4.7.1-rc1. As noted, I've seen this with xsd:string. I've tested xsd:dateTime and that outputs as expected. I've not tested any other datatypes.

kefo, 2016-12-22 20:31 UTC

I agree about the semantics. They're no different.

But to explain how this change cascades for us. A GET is performed on an fcrepo resource and the output copied to a triplestore. When loaded to the triplestore, the triplestore - rightly or wrongly or whatever - respects the datatype, such that you must include it when querying a literal/string.

Obviously, we'll reindex the triplestore when we update, but we also need to implement changes to various components because all of our SPARQL queries are designed with the datatype in mind. (I'm also inspecting any options with respect to the triplestore that might mitigate this.)

I'd be wary of all downstream products having fully adopted RDF 1.1 semantics and made requisite changes.

As for this behavior and fcrepo in 4.7.0, how the literal was inserted - with a declared datatype or not - seems immaterial. Esmé Cowles, this is the last thing I am going to say on this topic. I have made my point here, and I think we are simply not going to agree on this. From my perspective, Fedora has, since the very first 4.0.0 release, transacted in RDF 1.1: plain strings and typed string literals have always been treated as equivalent (both you and kefo have already noted this), and various RDF 1.1 syntaxes – syntaxes that are not part of RDF 1.0 – such as JSON-LD, Turtle, and the application/n-triples mimetype have also been part of Fedora 4.x from the beginning.
