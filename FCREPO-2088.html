FCREPO-2088 Move use of fcrepo-camel into a shared OSGi service in fcrepo-camel-toolbox

Componentsf4-camel
Affects versionsfcrepo-camel-toolbox 4.5.2
Fix versionsfcrepo-camel-toolbox-4.6.0

Description

Every fcrepo-camel component in fcrepo-camel-toolbox is configured separately, which leads to a lot of duplication of configuration. By converting the use of fcrepo-camel from a bean to an OSGi service, that configuration will all be centralized.
