Fixed in 6f500ad5bd71e22d5549833abb1b471235e96df5.

FYI, for Jetty 6.x, creating a contexts/fedora.xml with the following contents will do (substituting your own relative path to FEDORA_HOME for "fedora/default"):

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="ISO-8859-1"?>
<!DOCTYPE Configure PUBLIC "-//Mort Bay Consulting//DTD Configure//EN" "http://jetty.mortbay.org/configure.dtd">
<Configure class="org.mortbay.jetty.webapp.WebAppContext">
<Set name="contextPath">/fedora</Set>
<Set name="war"><SystemProperty name="jetty.home" default="."/>/webapps/fedora</Set>
<Set name="initParams">
<Map>
<Entry>
<Item>fedora.home</Item>
<Item><SystemProperty name="jetty.home" default="."/>/fedora/default</Item>
</Entry>
</Map>
</Set>
</Configure>
