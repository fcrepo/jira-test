Chris--

I'm going to come back at you on this one-- an integration with needs so complex that such filtering is interesting probably ought to be accomplishing it with an independent tool between the repository and consumers, e.g. Apache Camel or JBoss Drools or the like. Offering this kind of capability inside the repo is going to force duplication of efforts. At the very least, any such facility ought to be built into Fedora by reusing another open source product with the right capabilities (Camel would be high on the list).
