A. Soroka, 2016-09-29 13:31 UTC

Nothing wrong with using the EventBus in those modules in which it should be visible (just kernel-modeshape, I think) but otherwise, (e.g. in http-api) I don't think we should assume its existence. In that case, a strong alternative is to use Awaitility features with AtomicBoolean flags, or Semaphore instances, or other java.util.concurrent or java.util.concurrent.atomic features.
