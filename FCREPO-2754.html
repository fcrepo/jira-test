Description

ACLs may only be created at locations specified by the rel=acl Link header.  The working location for the acls is /path/to/resource/fcr:acl.  The task here is to ensure that users can successfully put to this location.  A separate ticket, https://jira.duraspace.org/browse/FCREPO-2743, ensures that users have permission before being able to perform this task.
