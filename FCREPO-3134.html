Description

At present, WebACs are handled via the same service which handles updating or creating of regular RDF source FedoraResources. There are some significant differences that are specific to webacs however, so it would make sense to split their handling into their own service. For instance, validation of the RDF body currently exists here:

https://github.com/fcrepo4/fcrepo4/blob/0c515260d353758e85336240628fb463bc9248da/fcrepo-kernel-impl/src/main/java/org/fcrepo/kernel/impl/services/AbstractService.java#L184

 

Persistence of a WebAC can most likely be treated the same as any rdf source.
