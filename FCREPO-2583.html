Review Memento, API spec, and Delta documents in order to come up with a design of how versioning and authorization interact in the modeshape impl, as consistent with the relevant specifications. Identity, answer, or escalate pertinent questions, such as:

  • When resources are versioned, are their ACLs implicitly versioned as well?
  • To what degree are the versions of a resource and the versions of its ACL independent?
  • Are ACLs for versioned resources immutable?
