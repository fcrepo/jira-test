Andrew Woods, 2015-09-17 17:36 UTC

This issue is also seen in the modeshape-4.4.0 upgrade branch:
https://github.com/fcrepo4/fcrepo4/tree/modeshape-4.4.0

To test on AWS, configure as documented here:
https://wiki.duraspace.org/display/FEDORA43/Deploying+a+Fedora+Cluster#DeployingaFedoraCluster-DeployinginAWS
...with the single change in /etc/default/tomcat7 as follows:

JAVA_OPTS="${JAVA_OPTS} -Dfcrepo.ispn.jgroups.configuration=default-configs/default-jgroups-ec2.xml"
#JAVA_OPTS="${JAVA_OPTS} -Dfcrepo.ispn.jgroups.configuration=jgroups-ec2.xml"

Also note, from a backwards compatibility perspective, clusters created from 4.3.0 have schema configurations that appear not to be parseable by the modeshape-4.4.0 upgrade branch.
