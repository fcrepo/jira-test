Description

Although the internal name of the time-map JCR node is "TimeMap", all interactions with time-maps (and mementos) will be served through /fcr:versions endpoints.
The FedoraResourceImpl.getPath() should be updated to replace occurrences of "/TimeMap" with "/fcr:versions".
