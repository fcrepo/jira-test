Description

After a Fedora relase, fcrepo4-vagrant is then tied to the SNAPSHOT version. However those artifacts are generally not recreated during the development cycle. Therefore there is no difference between the release and the post-release artifacts.

Therefore it makes sense that fcrepo4-vagrant should only be updated at a release of Fedora and left there until the next release.

Tagging Nick Ruest for thoughts?
