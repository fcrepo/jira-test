Description

This task is to document Fedora's approach to creating OCFL identifiers as an OCFL extension:
https://github.com/OCFL/extensions/issues/5

Considerations:

  • Identifier mapping varies depending on whether the Fedora resource is: an archival group, an atomic resource, an archival part, or a derivative resource (/fcr:acl, /fcr:metadata)
  • This should focus on what Fedora does, not sideloading (although a sideloading footnote may be appropriate)
