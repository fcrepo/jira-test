Description

When you click on "Create Version" for a resource (binary or container, named or unnamed version) in the Firefox web browser, the results page just shows the single word "false".

The URL in the navigation bar is "javascript:createVersionSnapshot()".

The version is created on the server, but some WARNings are thrown:
