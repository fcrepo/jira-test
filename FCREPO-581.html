Description

Reported by several people on the list, most recently Alex O'Neill and Paul Pound:

http://permalink.gmane.org/gmane.comp.cms.fedora-commons.devel/1450

Christian Rohrer has verified that this can be fixed by adding the following after the similar 127.0.0.1 line in the relevant XACML:

<AttributeValue DataType="http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#string">::1</AttributeValue>

This should allow local connections to API-M by default, regardless of whether the loopback ip address is specified in the IPv4 way, or the IPv6 way.
