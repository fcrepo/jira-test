Andrew Woods, 2017-07-12 20:11 UTC

I am happy to explore this issue with the underlying ModeShape team, but in any case, you are highly recommended to use a database instead of the file-store for your objects. The file-store is provided for simple testing, not production.

You may not, but if you have a scripted set of commands that reproduce this situation from an empty repository, it would likely make it easier for the ModeShape team to address the issue.

If, however, you plan on moving to a database backend, I would be inclined to lower the priority of this issue.

Josh Gum, 2017-07-12 20:14 UTC
