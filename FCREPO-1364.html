FCREPO-1364 Enable properties of InboundReferences

Description

This task is to create a new "Prefer" header type along the lines of:
http://fedora.info/definitions/v4/repository#InboundReferences

...that enables a client requesting GET on a container to indicated that the properties of InboundReferences are desired in the response.
See:
https://wiki.duraspace.org/display/FEDORA4x/RESTful+HTTP+API+-+Containers#RESTfulHTTPAPI-Containers-GETRetrievethecontentoftheresource

and:
https://github.com/fcrepo4/fcrepo4/blob/master/fcrepo-http-api/src/main/java/org/fcrepo/http/api/ContentExposingResource.java#L199
