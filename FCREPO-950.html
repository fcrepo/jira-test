A. Soroka, 2012-04-26 19:00 UTC

I think Jena is an interesting candidate for this use. It is fairly heavy, but you get quite a lot out of it. It is now an Apache incubating project, but I should be very surprised if it doesn't move through incubation quickly, since it is an old-established and richly-supported product. On the other hand, to my knowledge Jena isn't meant to fit as an interface over "foreign" SPARQL endpoints, which seems to be the intent of the products Chris mentions above.

One question then: do we commit to SPARQL as the defined interface from the RI to its triplestore? It's obviously (well, to me, anyway) the strongest candidate, especially in terms of flexibility (permitting folks to choose their own triplestore). Another possibility might be Sesame/OpenRDF Sail, which is of more limited scope (lives in a JVM world) but would probably be more efficient in operation.

This has some implications for 4.x, as well, for which architecture we're contemplating changing the relationships between the content bitstreams, the object package, and the RDF sea in which they swim.
