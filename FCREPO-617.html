Description

When the "other" option is selected during Fedora installation, the option to deploy local services is never presented. While Fedora can't deploy to "other" servlet containers, it should at least provide the local services (e.g. saxon, fop, imagemanip) webapps for manual deployment, just as it does for the the Fedora webapp.
