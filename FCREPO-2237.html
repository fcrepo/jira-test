Former user, 2016-09-27 00:15 UTC

I wonder if we're conflating two ideas here? To me an audit is about validation, e.g. by recalculating checksums and verifying that they match. I think it would be great if we could do that as part of the export/import and have that in a field in addition to those above (it could just be a boolean value or "checksum matches: true") but I want to make sure that that's what you mean.

If validating checksums is out of scope, then I think what Andrew Woods suggests above is fine (it should be easy to parse, which that would be, and I can't immediately think of any other elements I'd want)--but I wouldn't call it an audit log.
