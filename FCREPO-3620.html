FCREPO-3620 Fedora 5 -> 6 upgrade fails if a Fedora 5 export has retrieved the external binaries

Description

If you are moving from Fedora 5 to Fedora 6 and as part of this wanted to bring your external binaries into Fedora. The upgrade-util fails because it is expecting the "Content-Location" header to exist, but it doesn't because we redirect to the external service and get those headers instead.

https://github.com/fcrepo-exts/fcrepo-import-export/blob/310466bf32f80b60e7ad03a305b77a7a36ce9812/src/main/java/org/fcrepo/importexport/exporter/Exporter.java#L396-L398

This is probably an issue that affects both products but at the very least the import-export should get both the Fedora headers and the external service headers.
