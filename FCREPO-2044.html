FCREPO-2044 Remove fcr:backup and fcr:restore in favor of external scripts

Description

The endpoints fcr:backup and fcr:restore are unRESTful and expose implementation-specific serializations. They should be canned in favor of external scripts working against the repository HTTP API.

Andrew Woods, 2016-10-12 19:59 UTC

Although /fcr:backup and /fcr:restore are not a part of the Fedora Specification, they serve a very real and practical purpose in the community implementation. I would rather we not deprecate them until we have a time-tested alternative in the import-export utility.

Unknown, 2016-10-12 20:14 UTC

The import/export utility is orthogonal to this discussion. The backup/restore utility is entirely JCR specific. They do not belong in the HTTP API, and if they exist (I think they should), they should exist as part of a command line utility.
