Description

The modules in fcrepo-camel-toolbox follow the naming convention of:

<feature>-<backend>

for example:

audit-triplestore
indexing-solr

This ticket is to rename those so that they are all prefixed by fcrepo-:

fcrepo-audit-triplestore
fcrepo-indexing-solr

These all will continue to live in the org.fcrepo.camel groupId
