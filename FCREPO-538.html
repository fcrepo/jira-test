Colleagues,

The UK Research Councils are very keen to track all outputs deriving from our grants and are considering strengthening our OA position statements from publication in 'an appropriate repository' to a "repository which includes Funder and GrantReference fields"

Not being a Fedora user, I'm not clear whether these fields currently exist - and if so what format they have?

Can someone enlighten me please?
