Some things that immediately come to mind in the walk through include:

1. Determining the disposition of your FOXML files
If Fedora 3 is still running AND actively being updated, they shouldn't point to the foxml directories. If fedora will not be updated at all, maybe inform them about the id-resolution cache that will safe time on subsequent runs.
They'll need to find out (from their fedora 3 configuration) whether they're using legacy or akubra storage and configure appropriately.

2. Determine how things should look in the fedora 4 repository.
Tell them where to set the URL.
Tell them about the pidmapper, how to configure the default, and how they may wish to write their own.
