FCREPO-3157 Refactor PathNotFoundException and PathNotFoundRuntimeException

StatusOpen
PriorityMinor

Created2019-12-17 18:40 UTC
Updated2021-07-07 17:07 UTC
Affects versionsFedora 6.0.0

Description

Currently there are both PathNotFoundException and PathNotFoundRuntimeException, where the former is often rethrown as the latter. We can probably reduce down to just one of these.

May also be worth looking into if both RepositoryException and RepositoryRuntimeException are needed, since the former inherits from the latter.

If PathNotFoundException sticks around, it should inherit from RepositoryException.
