Description

As reported on the fc-users mailing list (http://thread.gmane.org/gmane.comp.cms.fedora-commons.user/5187), DefaultDOManager is logging WARNings on modifies for dsLocations that don't begin with a URL scheme. Looks like this change dates to r8450.

The warning could be useful to keep, but we should be excepting valid internal dsLocations.
