Attempting to PUT an abject into fedora with a url encoded slash in the identifier worked in fcrepo-docker 6.4.1 but has broken in 6.5.0. It seems that this is likely to do with the underlying tomcat which is pulled down from the official tomcat latest docker tag having changed something in the configuration.
