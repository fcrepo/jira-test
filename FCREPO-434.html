Description

Authentication – We will Shib-enable Fedora, enabling a
user to provide credentials managed by their origin
institution, and providing a single sign-in solution for
Fedora. We will obtain attributes about users
(Eduperson), and we will use those attributed in
authorization.
Authorization – we will support XML-encoded policies
(XACML). Fedora will constrain what can be expressed
in the policies to things Fedora is prepared to enforce.
Policies can refer to user attributes, and attributes of
Fedora digital objects. We will build the Fedora
authorization module to interpret and enforce policies.
