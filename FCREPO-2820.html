Description

Currently the Accept-Post header is missing on OPTIONS.  It must be present if POSTs allowed on LDPCvs. To verify the absence of the Accept-Post just perform an OPTIONS on an LDPCv 

curl -v -X OPTIONS http://localhost:8080/rest/versioned_resource/fcr:versions
