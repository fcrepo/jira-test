Some process notes:

Since we're not masochists, we won't craft our manifests by hand. bnd (or, in our case, bnd in its Maven plugin guise*) gives us a reasonably straightforward path to generate the required manifest entries. Sorting out the "correct" packages for export and import, writing an Activator for the bundle will require more effort.
