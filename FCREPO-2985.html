Description

Once the fcrepo-upgrde-utils skeleton is ready, this task is to add support for transforming WebAC representation from 4.7 format to 5.x format.  This will involve at the very least: 

  1. Merging group of authorizations associated with a 4.7.x style ACL into a single resource named fcr:acl that lives under the resource had the original 4.7.x ACL defined on it.  
  2. Remove the triple on the  protected resource that linked it to the top of the ACL authorization hierarchy.
