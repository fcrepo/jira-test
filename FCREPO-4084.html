fcrepo.ocfl.s3.timeout.connection.seconds is currently wired to a netty pool acquisition timeout. Should that instead be wired to a tcp connection timeout? (connectionTimeout in the builder)

connectionAcquisitionTimeout(Duration connectionAcquisitionTimeout)

The amount of time to wait when acquiring a connection from the pool before giving up and timing out.

connectionTimeout(Duration timeout)

The amount of time to wait when initially establishing a connection before giving up and timing out.
