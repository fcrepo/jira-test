XmlPullParserException, which is used by trippi which is used in the risearch interface in the fedora client recide in xpp3_min as shown by this stacktrace

Caused by: java.lang.NoClassDefFoundError: org/xmlpull/v1/XmlPullParserException
at org.trippi.TupleIterator.fromStream(TupleIterator.java:152)
at fedora.client.FedoraClient.getTuples(FedoraClient.java:681)
at net.sourceforge.ecm.repository.FedoraSoapImpl.query(FedoraSoapImpl.java:337)

Thus you cannot use the fedora client getTuples method until this jar is bundled.
