Comments (4)

Joshua Allan Westgard, 2017-06-07 22:06 UTC

Currently the verifier will fail for all external binaries on export. The tool calculates a SHA1 with the serialized binary on disk and the bare response from Fedora. It also does not catch that the Fedora resource is external in verifier.py, which is looking for the .external extension and therefore only works on excluding external resources on disk.
