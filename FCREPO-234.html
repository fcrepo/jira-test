FCREPO-234 MIMETypedStream finalizer prematurely closes

Description

MIMETypedStream's finalizer always tries to close the InputStream. In some cases, this can result in failed API-M operations due the stream being unexpectedly closed while it's being read(). This has been observed in higher load situations.

This is likely also an issue in the maintenance branch.
