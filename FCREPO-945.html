Description

The 3.5 rebuilder fails initialization during the spring bean loading phase. The problem is ServletContext is not in the classpath. Further investigations shows that the culprit is AttributeFinderModule#setServletContext(ServletContext cxt). This method seems to be superfluous, as the context is never used. Simply removing it fixes the problem.

Additionally, loading of akubra module fails due to the rebuilder looking for the llstore ModuleConfiguration rather than the Module itself.
