FCREPO-3858 Allow both ldp:hasMemberRelation and ldp:isMemberOfRelation for DirectContainers

TypeImprovement

Description

Fedora4 allowed DirectContainers to include both ldp:hasMemberRelation and ldp:isMemberOfRelation predicates. This allowed for convenient inverse properties: 

@prefix ldp:   <http://www.w3.org/ns/ldp#> .
@prefix schema: <http://schema.org/> .
<>
    ldp:hasMemberRelation   schema:associatedMedia ;
    ldp:isMemberOfRelation  schema:encodesCreativeWork ;
    .

F6 does not allow both failing with: Direct and Indirect containers must specify exactly one ldp:hasMemberRelation or ldp:isMemberOfRelation property, but multiple were present

Not sure if there is a technical reason these can no longer exist together

I am guessing that Fedora 4 did not follow the specification and it was implemented in Fedora 6 we tried to follow the specification closer (which was also easier, I think). Section 5.4.1.4 says

5.4.1.4 Each LDP Direct Container representation MUST contain exactly one triple whose subject is the LDPC URI, and whose predicate is either ldp:hasMemberRelation or ldp:isMemberOfRelation. The object of the triple is constrained by other sections, such as ldp:hasMemberRelation or ldp:isMemberOfRelation, based on the membership triple pattern used by the container.

We’ll bring this up at the November 3rd, 2022 tech call as hopefully the person that wrote this code can confirm.

Quinn Hart, 2022-11-09 21:35 UTC

So, it appears that all the commenting facility for the LDP are all moribund, so I don’t expect to a response on my query there. I’ll make a plea that if this isn’t hard technically, it’d be worth supporting. The example we gave above is one good use case, but Fedora 6 is making us rethink containment, in ways that would make this maybe more important for indirect containers. The basic idea for that is that archive groups are such a nice feature, we want to do a better job of dividing our archive into these groups. As a result, indirect containers are important to link other archival objects which can’t be contained, like collections of archival group items. 

@prefix ldp:   <http://www.w3.org/ns/ldp#> .
@prefix schema: <http://schema.org/> .
@prefix : <http://example.org/> .
<>  ldp:membershipResource <.>;
    ldp:isMemberOfRelation schema:partOf;
    ldp:XhasMemberRelation schema:hasPart;
    ldp:insertedContentRelation schema:hasPart;
    .

Here it’s really important that the collections know the items they havePart and equally, items know which collections they are partOf

And having to do that twice for both directions, kind of kills your bookkeeping on that.
