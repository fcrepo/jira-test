Renaud Waldura, 2009-02-04 20:36 UTC

This patch (attached) appears to fix the issue. (I.e. it works for me!)

It implements shutdownModule() in ConnectionPoolManagerImpl, which calls the new new method ConnectionPool.close().
ConnectionPool.close() merely forwards the call to dataSource.close().

Maybe the ddlConverter should be closed also?
