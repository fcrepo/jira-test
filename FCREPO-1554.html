Add the following auditTrail mappings to migration-utils:

modifyObject -> audit:resourceModification

modifyObject (checksum validation) -> premis:validation

modifyDatastreamByValue -> audit:contentModification/metadataModification

purgeDatastream -> audit:contentRemoval

Former user, 2015-05-26 15:07 UTC

The cryptographicHash terms are defined as both skos:Concept and owl:AnnotationProperty.

So I think it's fine to use them with the PREMIS predicates, and you'd need to have some kind of container if you had more than one of them. But you could also just use the cryptographicHash terms as predicates:

<object1> cryptofunc:sha1 "c91342b705b15cb4f6ac5362cc6a47d9425aec86"

IMHO, that's a lot simpler and allows you to have as many different checksums as you want without having to have a blanknode or other container to keep the algorithms and checksums matched up.
