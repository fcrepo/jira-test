FCREPO-1800 Do not add server-managed triples to hash URI pseudo-containers

Labelsapi-spec

Description

When hash URI fragments are created, they have a bunch of server-managed triples automatically attached to them. But hash URI fragments are intended to be lightweight portions of a resource, and the server-managed predicates aren't useful in that context.
