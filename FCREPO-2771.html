Bethany Seeger, 2018-06-22 20:46 UTC

Does anyone have suggestions on how to approach this? 

I see two ways: 

1)  change the subject after the data is retrieved from the lower level but before it is sent to client.  Ie, on a GET, the http layer would fetch the memento and change the subject then.  I am not a fan of this option. 

2) store the memento with the correct subject, but under the memento path, which may or may not function well, technically speaking, but seems the right way of doing this.   I'm not even sure this can be done... 

Thoughts? Other options? 

(Jared Whiklo, Ben Pennell)

 

Ben Pennell, 2018-06-22 21:20 UTC

Bethany Seeger

Fedora produces the subject based off the resource's internal path, so there's not really a subject stored that can be switched. Details are a little hazy at the moment, but I seem to recall that for binary's (or maybe just binary mementos) I had to remap the subject on the way out in order to get it to return the uri of the binary rather than of the description resource.

I believe it is taking place here, so not quite at the HTTP layer:
