The issue is not so simple, looking at the code.

It is not, per the underlying api, an error to remove a non-existing datastream. Rather, when you delete a datastream you get a list of the versions that got deleted. As such, there is no way of distinquishing between removing a datastream that does not exist, and attempting to remove an interval of versions that do not exist
