Description

For preservation purposes and continuity with past practices, fedora should attempt to keep on record a digest for all internal binaries.

OCFL generates and stores a digest internally (SHA-512) at the moment. Fedora should make use of this default digest and provide access to it. This may mean retrieving it via the OCFL client after write and storing to the .fcrepo sidecar file along with any other digests requested/provided by the client.
