Description

As a follow on ticket to https://jira.duraspace.org/browse/FCREPO-2748, https://jira.duraspace.org/browse/FCREPO-2749, and https://jira.duraspace.org/browse/FCREPO-2750, GET and HEAD requests to any external LDP-NR must correctly respond to the Want-Digest header defined in [RFC3230].

For context see https://fcrepo.github.io/fcrepo-specification/#external-content and https://fcrepo.github.io/fcrepo-specification/#redirect-and-proxy
