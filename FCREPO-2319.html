The stack traces are due to two things – one is related to ActiveMQ's osgi binding. Presumably that will be fixed in AMQ 5.15.x, but it is outside our control. The other is related to the structure of our integration testing – repeatedly starting and stoping Fedora – sometimes consuming messages, sometimes not. The best solution to that, IMO is to move all integration testing into a single Pax Exam environment. That means either completely removing iTests from fcrepo-camel-toolbox and pushing those into fcrepo-camel-tests or merging those two projects (this would be my preference, but it would also involve switching to using Gradle as a build tool, as I have for this project: https://gitlab.amherst.edu/acdc/repository-extension-services – the reason has to do with the fact that pax exam testing needs the artifacts to be installed to the local maven repo before they can be tested, but maven requires the testing phase to be before the installation phase – gradle is a lot more flexible about such things)
