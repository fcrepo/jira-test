FCREPO-139 METS Deserializer: evaluate use of RELS-INT

Description

The METS deserializer automatically creates a RELS-INT datastream and puts association information about AMDIDs and DMDIDs within it.

Is this what we want long-term? Does the actual use of RELS-INT here agree with our imagined use of it (if any) in the future? Resolving this issue should result in either:

a) changing the way this is done for the METS deserializer (losing the information?)

b) documenting this reserved use of RELS-INT somewhere outside the code.
