Aaron Birkland, 2008-10-07 22:16 UTC

It looks like the authz problem is ultimately due to a missing policy upon journal replay. Policy 'permit-if-owner.xml' matched during the live run, but wasn't present in the journal replay run. Looks like this policy (among others) is loaded at runtime, then later removed as part of a junit test. Since policies may be stored/managed externally, they don't fall under the journalling umbrella.

Thus, I'm wondering if policy evaluation is appropriate for journal replay at all. Since the existence of a journal entry implies that an operation successfully passed the policy evaluation, there really is no point in re-evaluating the policies upon replay. At best, it will succeed at slightly increasing the entropy of the universe. At worst, fail (as we've seen). Thus, it may be worthwhile to see if it's easy to disable policy evaluation at journal replay... that would be the most straightforward thing to do.
