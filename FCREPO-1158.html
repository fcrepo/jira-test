FCREPO-1158 PolicyIndexRebuilder fails with a NullPointerException in FCRepo 3.6

Fix versionsFedora 3.6.2

Description

The PolicyIndexRebuilder does not have access to the PolicyIndex it needs, because the RebuildServer does not load the requisite Spring beans. This leads to a NullPointerException. It can be worked around by copying the PolicyIndex configuration, and configuring an additional bean for the rebuilder like so:

<bean id="org.fcrepo.server.security.xacml.pdp.data.PolicyIndexRebuilder2"
class="org.fcrepo.server.security.xacml.pdp.data.PolicyIndexRebuilder" >
<property name="policyIndex"
ref="org.fcrepo.server.security.xacml.pdp.data.PolicyIndex" />
</bean>
