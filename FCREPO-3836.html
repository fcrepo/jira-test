FCREPO-3836 Migration-utils generate invalid RDF triple if xml:lang is present

Description

Currently when performing a Fedora 3 to 6 migration a block in the RELS-EXT (which is added as properties to the object) like this

<cc:useGuidelines xmlns:cc="http://web.resource.org/cc/" xml:lang="en">This resource is free of copyright restrictions and is published under the Public Domain Mark 1.0.</cc:useGuidelines>

is transformed into

<info:fedora/some-resource> <http://web.resource.org/cc/useGuidelines> "This resource is free of copyright restrictions and is published under the Public Domain Mark 1.0."@en^^<http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#langString> .

This is invalid RDF as you can only have a language on a Plain Literal. Because language is probably important this triples should instead be

<info:fedora/some-resource> <http://web.resource.org/cc/useGuidelines> "This resource is free of copyright restrictions and is published under the Public Domain Mark 1.0."@en .
