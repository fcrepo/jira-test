FCREPO-3523 Can put a ghost node over a tombstone

Description

While you can't create a new resource on top of a tombstone, you can create a resource as a child of it.

Example

> curl -i -ufedoraAdmin:fedoraAdmin http://localhost:8080/rest/box
HTTP/1.1 410 Gone
Date: Fri, 06 Nov 2020 21:00:20 GMT
Set-Cookie: JSESSIONID=node018i1ge7u1mdut1seew79r98hxf4.node0; Path=/
Expires: Thu, 01 Jan 1970 00:00:00 GMT
Set-Cookie: rememberMe=deleteMe; Path=/; Max-Age=0; Expires=Thu, 05-Nov-2020 21:00:20 GMT
Link: <http://localhost:8080/rest/box/fcr:tombstone>; rel="hasTombstone"
Content-Type: text/plain
Content-Length: 79
Server: Jetty(9.4.24.v20191120)
