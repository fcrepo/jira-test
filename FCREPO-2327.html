Description

Repository resources that make use of the external redirect feature of Fedora will appear in serialized form on disk as a zero-length binary. FCREPO-2296 Closed will implement the addition of the extension ".external" to all such resources in order to mark them as redirects.

The task of this ticket is to make import-export-verify handle such resources (i.e. recognize the extension, and skip over checksum verification since the resource is not part of the exported set),
