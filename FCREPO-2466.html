Description

When doing a build with parallelization turned on (e.g., mvn -Dfcrepo.streaming.parallel=true clean install), I am seeing intermittent failures in these three ITs:

FedoraLdpIT.testRoundTripReplaceGraph:831 expected:<204> but was:<500>
