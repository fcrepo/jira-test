Currently when making a request to a URI in fedora that does not exist, it returns a 404 response with the message

"Error: No OCFL mapping found for info:fedora/somenode"

OCFL seems irrelevant at this tier of the platform, not to mention its showing the internal fedora id. It would be good to give a cleaner message back that does not include these two things, maybe just "not found"
